Game notes

    How to play

    Choose two of your six cards for the dealer’s crib. During pegging, tap a card to play it without passing 31. Say go when no card fits. Fifteen and thirty-one score 2; pairs score 2, 6, or 12; runs score their length. The last card scores 1 unless it makes 31. Then hands score in order: nondealer, dealer, dealer’s crib. Each fifteen scores 2, pairs 2, runs their length, and a four-card flush scores 4 (5 with matching starter). A jack matching the starter suit scores 1. First to 121 wins.
